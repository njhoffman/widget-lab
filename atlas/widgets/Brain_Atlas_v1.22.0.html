Neural Evolution Atlas

Version history

Major changes across atlas releases.

1.10 CurrentOct 16, 2026
  • Show no more than two nested anatomical outlines: the selected region and its immediate subdivision, while keeping broad division shading.
  • Move geological context and selected region labels into the brain map header in place of the schematic-atlas title.
  • Integrate the compact clickable evolutionary window into the brain map header, removing the separate timeline panel.
  • Match the timeline reset button height to the range control across desktop and mobile layouts.
1.9 Oct 16, 2026
  • Collapsed evolutionary filtering into a compact clickable range strip with a detailed slider/presets dialog and one-touch reset.
  • Linked individual brain regions to their participating circuits and networks, complementing existing system-to-region navigation.
  • Added axial composite schematic projection and clickable empty-space deselection.
  • Improved major-division territories and cortical lobe highlighting with shaped SVG outlines; made region and connection strokes thinner.
  • Condensed region/system counts and hid empty selection metrics.
1.8 Oct 16, 2026
  • Split large-scale functional networks from anatomical circuits into their own top-level category, bringing the hierarchy to six primary divisions.
  • Replaced system-type chips on hierarchy entries with estimated appearance ages in Ma, matching region entries.
  • Re-clicking any active primary division now collapses it without scrolling, while a repeated tap on an individual system or region still jumps to its details when off-screen.
1.7Oct 16, 2026
  • Added Circuits & networks as a fifth primary hierarchy category, with 16 documented systems.
  • Draws thin directional anatomical pathways and subtle two-way functional network links; highlights participating region extents without subgroup background washes.
  • Re-tapping a selected item scrolls to its information when off-screen; expanded orbitofrontal cortex context.
1.6Oct 15, 2026
  • Changed the numbering convention to major.minor, with the new release labeled 1.6.
  • Made the version label open an expandable history of updates.
1.5Oct 14, 2026 · formerly v5
  • Added 20 regions (37 → 57) across brainstem, thalamus, basal forebrain, and association cortex.
  • Removed the older/younger legend beneath the evolutionary timeline.
  • Refined anatomical navigation and narrow-screen layouts.
1.4Oct 14, 2026 · formerly v4
  • Added nucleus accumbens as the 37th region.
  • Moved version and update date into the top-right release bar.
  • Combined timeline boundaries into a single geological date-range subtitle.
1.3Oct 14, 2026 · formerly v3
  • Separated cortical and subcortical forebrain; organized cortex by lobe.
  • Introduced dimmed nonselected divisions and soft subdivision highlights.
  • Added 11 regions and evolutionary adaptation explanations; removed timeline milestone markers.
1.2Oct 14, 2026 · formerly v2
  • Compact hierarchical browser, initially collapsed, with division and subgroup selection.
  • Segmented geologic history by period and Cenozoic epoch.
  • Expanded evolutionary, animal-homology, neurotransmitter, and clinical details.
1.1Oct 14, 2026 · formerly v1
  • Introduced the interactive schematic brain and 25 selectable regions.
  • Added evolutionary filtering, geological date labels, and region information panels.
