Sources, cautions & references ▾
Data provenance: Shared Taxa v1.0 (2026-10-15) is embedded at build time. Original Tree of Life educational parentage is preserved; 130 additional concepts from Deep Time are searchable but unplaced. Figures and evolutionary ages retain source interpretations and unresolved scientific disagreements. Scope & taxonomic cautions: The kingdom headings follow the seven-kingdom listing used by ITIS (Animalia, Plantae, Fungi, Chromista, Protozoa, Bacteria and Archaea), not a single universally accepted phylogenetic scheme. Kingdoms such as Protozoa—and the historical circumscription of Chromista—do not map neatly onto modern monophyletic branches; the cladogram here is an educational navigation scaffold, not a complete species-level phylogeny. The title counts only the entries indexed in this atlas (including indexed fossil lineages). Subdivision badges show approximate extant-only richness or reference inventory counts, regardless of whether extinct branches are displayed. The worldwide rank inventories are approximations or lower bounds assembled from different classification systems; they should not be mistaken for an exhaustive, precisely synchronized extant census. Species counts from Catalogue of Life include some fossil records, so rounded living diversity estimates are used in the badges instead. No accepted global count exists for unranked clades, so that badge reports only named clades represented in this atlas that have surviving members. A dash means no reliable worldwide count was supplied for that rank. Population estimates in species cards are separate and are dated, unit-labelled estimates rather than current censuses. Fossil and origin dates are approximate, and a lineage's hypothetical origin must not be confused with its earliest certain fossil. Images are fetched, when expanded, from Wikimedia-hosted Wikipedia page images and depend on connectivity, availability, and their individual licenses.
