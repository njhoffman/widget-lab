# Source ambiguities and reconciliation warnings

**Scope:** This is a lossless export of Tree of Life Explorer v1.7's effective built-in dataset, *not* a reconciled Shared Taxa canonical taxonomy. Preserve these issues during merging.

1. **Original root:** `Life` exists as a hidden application-level root although the UI starts with the three domains. It is exported with its own ID and parent `null`; the 237 displayed/indexed descendants remain separate.
2. **Animalia late mutation:** Initially `Animalia` is declared with parent `null` and diversity text `>1.5 million described`; later the script changes its parent to `Opisthokonta` and its count text to `1,796,900 species listed in Catalogue of Life (Sept 2026 XR)`. The export records both declared and effective values, with the effective hierarchy as `parent_id`.
3. **IDs are local UI keys:** The source uses human-readable taxon names (sometimes `_domain` suffix) as identifiers, not Life Science Identifiers, NCBI, GBIF, WoRMS, or Catalogue of Life taxon IDs. Treat them as **source-stable**, not global canonical IDs. Do not silently rename during merges.
4. **Kingdom/classification overlap:** Some seven-kingdom category nodes (especially `Protozoa` and `Chromista`) are not monophyletic across modern trees; some `Clade` nodes overlap ranked taxa, and evolutionary relationships including very deep animal branches are contested. Reconciliation should retain provenance and alternate classifications.
5. **Source ranks carry extinct status:** A few original `rank` strings start with `Extinct`, while other extinction status is derived from `end`. Both were preserved, with an additional derived `extinct` boolean; retain original rank text separately from standardized rank.
6. **Ages are estimates:** `age` is the atlas's approximate origin in Ma, not consistently the earliest fossil, stem age, or crown age. `end` may be a rounded terminal fossil occurrence. Do not convert into hard extinction/origin dates without additional evidence.
7. **Subdivision totals are not census data:** `WORLD_COUNTS` combines mixed-source extant-focused estimates, inequalities, and approximate order-of-magnitude counts; some category totals are conservative lower bounds. Numeric strings such as `≥7.5k`, `>1.5m` and `≈25k–32k` are intentionally retained as strings, not coerced to point estimates.
8. **`count` is free text:** Individual taxa's `count` may express species richness, individuals, qualitative descriptions, or include dated Catalogue of Life totals with fossil listings. It is preserved verbatim in `taxon_details.json` and `raw_source_fields`, rather than parsed into a misleading single metric.
9. **Population-unit inconsistencies:** `POPULATIONS` mixes global wild animals, surveyed regional totals, mature adults, colony counts, seasonal migration estimates, and one explicitly unknown species estimate with genus-level cell abundance (Prochlorococcus). Scope, note, source, URLs, and estimate text are preserved verbatim; no unit conversions.
10. **Organism records are samples, not all unique species:** Some entries are common names, aggregate categories, living representatives of ancient lineages, or fossil groups. The source `taxon` pointer refers to the selected atlas branch, not necessarily the exact organism's taxon.
11. **Images are runtime-resolved:** Most examples do not have static image files or validated stable image URLs. The widget loads Wikipedia REST/MediaWiki thumbnails with optional Wikimedia Commons file-name fallbacks. The export records all image references and generated fallback URLs, but makes **no verified-working-image claim**. Check image licensing/attribution during migration.
12. **References are primarily broad:** Footer references apply to the atlas's concepts, not each record. Population records have more specific URLs; missing per-taxon citations must not be fabricated.
13. **Time boundaries depend on source vintage:** `geologic_time.json` preserves the exact intervals and numerical boundaries in this widget. Deep Time Atlas may contain different revisions, subdivisions, or terminological choices; reconcile explicitly against shared standard.
14. **Redundant and potentially mismatched records:** Many representative organisms correspond to species not independently indexed at species rank; external Wikipedia article titles and `scientificName(...)` UI heuristics should not be promoted to authoritative taxonomy without checking.
15. **Dates and versioning:** v1.7 only adds the export and changelog; the scientific content is unchanged from source v1.6 and is intentionally not reverified for October 2026.
